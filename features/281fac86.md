# sweep_retention low-value exemption at every stage (task 281fac86)

ROOT_CAUSE: `sweep_retention` gated only STEP 1 (active→archived) on low-value
status; steps 2 (archived→pending_delete) and 3 (pending_delete→hard-delete) did
not. So a doc that reached `archived` by ANY other path — notably a deliberate
`set_lifecycle(ext, "archived")` on an owned CANONICAL — could drift down the
eviction chain and hard-delete after the 270+30d windows. Default-off (no live
impact), but a real least-surprise footgun: a manual archive is a keep-hidden, not
a delete-queue. (review-a46b23cb notice.)

## FIX
Factor the low-value predicate into one `low_value = "status IN
('stale','duplicate','superseded')"` clause and apply it at EVERY stage (step 1,
2, and 3). A manually-archived canonical (status stays 'canonical') now fails the
filter at step 2 and stays archived forever — matching the records/pinned-exempt
spirit and "down-rank, don't delete". Only genuinely low-value docs walk the full
chain.
